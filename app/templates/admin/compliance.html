Compliance & retention

Because one competition format works legally, it doesn't follow that every new format does. A competition can only go live once every mechanic it uses has been signed off by the person responsible for the business/legal side. New wheels, scratch games, draw mechanics or promotional formats added later start unsigned and can't be published until this is done. Full checklist: docs/NEW-MECHANIC-CHECKLIST.md.
