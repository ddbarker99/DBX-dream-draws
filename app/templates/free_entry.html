Free postal entry

You can enter any {{ config.SITE_NAME }} prize draw or instant win game free by post. A valid postal entry goes into the same draw, and can win instant prizes, on exactly the same basis as a paid entry.

1. Write your entry

On a postcard, or a letter in an envelope, write clearly:

  • the name of the competition you're entering (exactly as shown on its page)
  • your answer to that competition's question
  • your full name and postal address
  • your email address — use the one on your {{ config.SITE_NAME }} account if you have one
  • your phone number and date of birth

2. Post it

Send it by ordinary first- or second-class post — you don't need recorded or tracked delivery — to:

{% if config.POSTAL_ADDRESS %}{{ config.POSTAL_ADDRESS }}{% else %}Our postal entry address — {% if config.SUPPORT_EMAIL %}email {{ config.SUPPORT_EMAIL }}{% else %}contact us{% endif %} and we'll send it to you straight away.{% endif %}

3. Deadline

Your entry must arrive before the competition's closing time, shown on its page. An entry that arrives on the closing day counts as on time. Allow a few days for the post — proof of posting isn't proof that we received it.

What happens next

  • We record every envelope we open, with the date it arrived and the outcome.
  • If it's valid, we allocate a ticket number at random and email it to you.
  • If your email matches a {{ config.SITE_NAME }} account, the entry appears in My account → My entries, and any cash or site-credit instant prize goes straight to that wallet (for instant win games, when you reveal your play). You don't need an account to enter by post — without one, we contact you about any prize.

Limits

  • One entry per envelope. You can send more than one envelope.
  • Postal and online entries count together towards each competition's per-person limit, shown on its page.
  • The daily free game is already free, so it doesn't take postal entries.

When an entry can't be accepted

We'll record the envelope but can't issue a ticket if:

  • it arrives after the competition has closed;
  • the answer to the question is wrong or missing;
  • the entrant is under 18 or doesn't live in the UK;
  • the entrant has already reached the per-person limit;
  • the competition has sold out before it arrives; or
  • it's incomplete or illegible, so we can't tell which competition it's for or who sent it.

Full details are in section 4 of our terms and conditions.
