Free postal entry

You can enter any of our competitions for free. Postal entries go into the draw — and can win instant prizes — on exactly the same basis as paid entries.

How to enter

  1. On a postcard or in a letter, write: the competition name, your answer to the question, your full name, address, email, phone number and date of birth.
  2. Post it, first or second class, to:
    {% if config.POSTAL_ADDRESS %}{{ config.POSTAL_ADDRESS }} {% else %}our postal entry address — {% if config.SUPPORT_EMAIL %}email {{ config.SUPPORT_EMAIL }}{% else %}contact us{% endif %} and we'll send it to you straight away.{% endif %}
  3. One entry per envelope. It must arrive before the competition closes.

We'll email you your ticket number once your entry has been added. Postal and online entries count together towards the per-person limit, and the same eligibility rules apply: UK residents aged 18 or over.

Things to know

  • Ordinary first- or second-class post is all you need — you don't need recorded or tracked delivery.
  • Each competition's question is shown on its page. Answer it on your entry.
  • Incomplete, illegible, late or duplicated entries can't be accepted. Proof of posting isn't proof of receipt.
  • Free entries go into the same draw as paid entries and can win instant prizes on exactly the same basis.

Full details are in section 4 of our terms and conditions.
