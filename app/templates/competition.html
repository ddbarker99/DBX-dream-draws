FAQs

{% if c.game_type %}
Does the animation decide whether I win?

No. Results were decided and sealed before the game went live. The scratch, spin or box just reveals the result already linked to your play.

What if I close the page before revealing?

Nothing is lost. Your plays wait in your account, and any prize is paid automatically after 24 hours.

{% else %}
When will I know if I've won?

Instant prizes show as soon as your numbers are confirmed. The main draw result appears on this page at the draw time and we email the winner straight away.

What if it doesn't sell out?

The draw still goes ahead at the time shown — your odds are simply better.

{% endif %}
Where can I see my {{ unit }}s?

On your confirmation page, in your confirmation email, and any time under My account → My entries.
