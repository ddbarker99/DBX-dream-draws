Every eligible entry — paid or free — had exactly one equal chance. The draw only looks at ticket numbers; it doesn't know who owns a ticket or how it was entered.

Check it yourself

  1. Download the frozen entry list (every eligible ticket number, in order, separated by commas).
  2. Its SHA-256 fingerprint should be the entries hash below.
  3. The SHA-256 fingerprint of the revealed secret should be the seed hash we published before sales opened.
    4. {% if last.method == 'redraw' %}
  4. For this redraw, the list is the original frozen list with every earlier winning ticket removed. Work out HMAC-SHA256(secret, "entries hash:redraw:{{ draws|length }}"), read it as a number and take the remainder after dividing by the number of entries. That position (counting from 0) is the winning ticket: position {{ last.winning_index }} → #{{ last.winning_number }}.
    5. {% else %}
  5. Work out HMAC-SHA256(secret, entries hash), read it as a number and take the remainder after dividing by the number of entries. That position (counting from 0) in the list is the winning ticket: position {{ last.winning_index }} → #{{ last.winning_number }}.
    6. {% endif %}
