About {{ config.SITE_NAME }}

{{ config.SITE_NAME }} is a UK-based online prize competition platform. We run prize draws for cash, tech and other prizes, plus instant win games with real cash prizes you can withdraw.

Our aim is simple: make entering a competition clear, fair and enjoyable from the moment you land on the site to the moment the winner is announced.

What we stand for

  • Clear information. Every competition shows the prize, ticket price, maximum entries, closing date and draw date before you enter.
  • Fair draws you can check. Every draw is provably fair — the result is locked in before tickets go on sale and anyone can verify it afterwards.
  • Real cash prizes. Cash winnings go to your cash balance and can be withdrawn to your bank or PayPal.
  • A free entry route. Every competition can be entered free by post with the same chance of winning.
  • Safer play. Spending limits and breaks are built into every account. Responsible play.

Our mission

To build {{ config.SITE_NAME }} into a trusted UK prize competition brand, with exciting prizes, straightforward entry, transparent draws and reliable customer support — whether it's your first competition or your fiftieth.
