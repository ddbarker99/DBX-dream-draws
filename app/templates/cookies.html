Cookie policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains how {{ S }} uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

Our approach

We keep cookies to the minimum needed to run the website securely and provide the services you ask for. We do not use advertising, analytics or behavioural-tracking cookies, so there is nothing to accept or reject.

Strictly necessary cookie

NamePurposeDuration
sessionKeeps you logged in, remembers your basket and protects website forms and account functions against misuse.Up to 30 days, or until you log out or clear it

Strictly necessary cookies are needed for functions you request and are used without consent, as the law allows. They can't be switched off on our website without those functions stopping working.

Similar technologies

Our website registers a small "service worker" script so that it can be added to your phone's home screen like an app. It does not store personal information, track you or cache your account pages.

Payment provider

When you pay, you are taken to Stripe's secure payment page. Stripe may use its own cookies or similar technologies for payment processing, authentication, security and fraud prevention. Stripe's own privacy and cookie information applies there.

External resources

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Google Fonts does not set cookies, but — like any website request — your browser sends technical information such as your IP address to Google when the fonts are downloaded. Our Privacy Policy explains more generally how service providers may process personal information.

Non-essential cookies

If we ever introduce analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies that require consent, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before they are used.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block or delete the session cookie, parts of {{ S }} will stop working correctly — for example, you won't be able to stay logged in, keep a basket or use your account.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if our website technology or legal obligations change. The latest version always shows its last-updated date.
