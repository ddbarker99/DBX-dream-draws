# Pre-release compliance checklist for a new competition mechanic

Because one competition format works legally, it does **not** follow that every new format does. A new wheel, scratch
game, draw mechanic, bonus round or promotional format can change the legal character of the whole product (for
example, turning a prize competition into an unlicensed lottery or gaming).

**The rule:** no competition using a mechanic can be published until the person responsible for the business/legal side
has signed it off in **Admin → More → Compliance & retention**. The site enforces this — the publish checks include
*"Mechanic signed off for compliance"*. Mechanics added to the code later start unsigned.

## Before developers start building
Answer on the Development board (the four standard questions) **and**:
1. Which legal structure is it meant to sit in? (e.g. prize competition with a free entry route; free draw)
2. Is there a free entry route with an equal chance of winning, and is it as easy to find as the paid route?
3. Once accepted, are paid and free entries treated identically by the winner-selection system?
4. If a skill or knowledge element is relied on, is it genuine and not trivially easy?
5. Is every result decided by a method that is fixed and published before sales open, and can't be changed afterwards?
6. Does anything about it look or feel like gambling (stakes, repeated rapid play, near-miss effects, chasing losses)?
   If yes, take advice before going further.
7. What changes to the terms, competition conditions, FAQs and marketing are needed?
8. Which responsible-play protections apply (spending limits, breaks, self-exclusion, age checks)?

## Before it goes live (the sign-off form)
All eight boxes on the sign-off form must be confirmed, a named person must take responsibility, and the note must say
how the mechanic fits the legal structure and what advice was taken (who, when). The sign-off is permanent — a change of
mind is recorded as a new sign-off, never an edit.

## Existing mechanics
Mechanics that were in use before this checklist existed are marked **"Existing — review recommended"**. They keep
working, but the owner should review each one with their solicitor and record a proper sign-off.

See also `docs/MECHANICS.md` (how each current mechanic works) and the Gambling Commission's guidance on prize
competitions and free draws.
